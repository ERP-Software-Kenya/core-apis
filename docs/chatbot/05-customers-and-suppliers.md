# Customers and Suppliers

## Customers

A customer is a person or business that purchases goods through the ERP. Customers can be linked to bills, orders, quotations, and credit accounts.

### Customer Types

- **Regular**: Standard returning customer.
- **New**: First-time or occasional buyer.
- **Shop**: A retail shop buying in bulk.
- **Big Customer**: High-volume or key-account customer. May receive special pricing or extended credit.

### Customer Credit

Customers can have a credit limit. When a Credit Sale is made, the customer's credit balance is debited. The credit balance must be repaid before further credit sales are allowed (subject to the configured limit).

Credit approvals: when a sale exceeds the customer's credit limit, a Credit Approval Request is created and must be approved by an authorised user before the sale can proceed.

### Walk-In Customers

A bill can be created without a pre-existing customer record by entering a walk-in name and phone number. Walk-in details are stored on the bill for reference.

## Suppliers

A supplier is a business from which the organisation purchases stock.

### Supplier Record

Contains: name, contact details, address, and associated products. Each product can be linked to one or more suppliers.

### Using Suppliers

When creating a Purchase Order, select a supplier. The supplier's details (name, contact) are copied onto the PO for reference and communication.

### Purchase Returns

Purchase returns are linked to the supplier and the original PO. They record the return of defective or incorrect stock and the expected credit from the supplier.
