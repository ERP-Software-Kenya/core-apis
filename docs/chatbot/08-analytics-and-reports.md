# Analytics and Reports

## Analytics Overview

The Analytics section provides real-time summaries of business performance across sales, purchases, and inventory.

## Sales Analytics

- **Revenue This Month**: Total value of completed bills in the current calendar month.
- **Bills This Month**: Count of completed bills in the current month.
- **Average Bill Value**: Revenue divided by bill count.
- **Top Products**: Products with the highest sales volume or revenue in the selected period.
- **Sales by Location**: Breakdown of revenue per warehouse or store.

## Purchase Analytics

- **Spend This Month**: Total cost of Purchase Orders in Received, Partially Allocated, or Allocated status in the current month.
- **Top Suppliers**: Suppliers with the highest order value.
- **Purchase Volume**: Number of POs created or received.

## Inventory Analytics

- **Low Stock**: Products at any location where quantity on hand is below the configured low-stock threshold.
- **Stock Valuation**: Total value of all stock on hand across all locations, calculated as quantity × average unit cost.
- **Stock Movement History**: Log of all stock additions, deductions, transfers, and adjustments.

## Reports

### Invoice / Bill PDF Export

A PDF invoice can be exported for any completed bill. Go to the bill, click "Export PDF". The PDF includes customer details, line items, taxes, totals, and payment information.

### Report Generation Log

The system maintains a log of all generated reports. Admins can view when each report was created, by whom, and download previous exports.

## Filtering Analytics

Most analytics screens support filtering by:

- **Date range**: Select a custom start and end date.
- **Location**: Filter to a specific warehouse or store.
- **Branch**: Filter to all locations within a branch.

Org-wide users see all branches. Branch managers see only their branch's data.
