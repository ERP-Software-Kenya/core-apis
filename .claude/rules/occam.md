Occam: every claim, checklist item, example, sub-section, or scope addition in any artifact (plan, checklist, README, recommendation, code, taxonomy) must trace to specific evidence — a contract clause, observation, prior decision, or tool output — citable on demand.
Ban hand-wave enumerations: 'etc.', 'or similar', 'and others', speculative example lists. Replace with verification instructions or omit.
Ban prophylactic items: structure, checkboxes, branches, configs added for risks not yet observed. Add when observed.
Prefer flat lists; categories only above ~10 items and only when grouping changes reader behavior.
Hedges ('might', 'could', 'possibly') without specific conditions are un-evidenced claims wearing humility — cut or commit.
When in doubt: cut. Restoring later is cheap; removing later is what generates this rule.
